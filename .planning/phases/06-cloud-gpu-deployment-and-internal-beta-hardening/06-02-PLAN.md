Harden persistence and readiness for the separated internal beta services.

Purpose: make the API and worker prove cheap dependencies before accepting beta traffic and preserve mounted audio/job persistence outside app processes.
Output: readiness routes, worker health surface, and persistence/readiness tests.

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Health caller -> service internals | Health endpoints expose only dependency status, not storage paths or payload data. |
| Mounted storage -> playback routes | Storage paths remain server-side; browser receives controlled playback URLs only. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-02-01 | Denial of service | readiness checks | medium | mitigate | Keep checks cheap and prohibit model warmup/provider synthesis/transcription in readiness. |
| T-06-02-02 | Tampering | storage readiness | medium | mitigate | Verify the configured storage root is writable and path-contained through existing job services. |
| T-06-02-03 | Information disclosure | health responses | medium | mitigate | Return check names and safe status messages only; do not expose raw storage paths or payload data. |
</threat_model>
